Notice & deposit refund

A minimum 30-day notice period applies. If notice is less than 15 days, one month’s rent is adjusted; with 15–30 days’ notice, half a month’s rent is adjusted and the balance refunded.

Required documents

Bring your identity and address proof, emergency contact details, and employment or study particulars as applicable.

House rules

Review the handbook for visitor registration, shared-space care, security and other resident guidelines. Read all house rules.

FAQ

A few things you might be wondering.

What is the monthly rent?

Twin sharing is ₹13,000 per person per month, and three sharing is ₹13,000 per person per month.

What does the rent include?

Food, AC usage, Wi-Fi, water and washing machine use are included. Contact management for deposit details and any other applicable charges.

Do rooms have attached bathrooms?

Yes, all rooms have attached bathrooms and air conditioning.

Are there AC usage timings?

There is no timing restriction on AC usage.

Is food provided?

Yes, vegetarian and non-vegetarian food is provided. Contact management for the menu and meal timings.

Can residents cook?

Yes, residents can use the shared kitchen and utensils for cooking.

Is parking available?

Yes, car and two-wheeler parking is available. Contact management about space availability and any applicable conditions.

When is the opening?

The opening is scheduled for 8 November 2026.
