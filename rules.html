Hostel Rules & Regulations

Resident Handbook, Admission Terms and Acknowledgement for PV Associates Women's Hostel.
Effective Date: 1st November 2026 | Approved by: Prema Parthasarathy

1. Eligibility and admission

Admission is for women aged 18 or above, subject to availability. A completed admission form, identity and address proof, emergency contact details, and employment or study particulars as applicable are required. Management may verify documents and will handle personal information confidentially.

2. Occupancy and room allocation

Occupy only the assigned bed/room. Subletting, bed transfers and overnight accommodation of unregistered persons are prohibited. Room changes require management approval. Permitted occupancy and sharing arrangements will be communicated before admission.

3. Rent, deposit and receipts

The signed occupancy form records the accommodation charge, deposit, food charges, any separate electricity/AC charges, payment due date and accepted payment method. Receipts or electronic acknowledgements will be issued. No additional charge may be imposed unless disclosed and agreed or permitted by law.

4. Payment default

Inform management promptly about delayed payments. Any late fee must be stated in the signed agreement and comply with law. Non-payment is handled through written communication and lawful remedies; belongings will not be seized and entry will not be unlawfully restricted.

5. Entry, exit and overnight absence

Use the designated entrance and cooperate with entry logging. Notify the warden about late returns or overnight absence where reasonably practical. Adult residents retain freedom of movement; security procedures must not become an unlawful curfew or confinement.

6. Visitors and deliveries

Visitors must register at reception/security and meet residents in designated areas during posted visiting hours. Access to accommodation floors/rooms requires specific authorisation. Deliveries remain in the designated delivery area. Management may refuse entry where there is a credible safety risk.

7. Behaviour and respect

Treat residents, staff and neighbours respectfully. Harassment, bullying, threats, violence, discriminatory conduct and unlawful activity are prohibited. Keep noise low, particularly during notified quiet hours. Complaints are assessed fairly and confidentially.

8. Cleanliness, laundry and waste

Keep rooms, attached toilets, dining and common areas hygienic. Segregate waste and use designated bins. Do not obstruct drainage or put unsuitable waste in toilets or sinks. Use designated laundry and drying spaces.

9. Food, kitchen and dining

Meal plans, charges, timings and exclusions are communicated separately. Observe dining hygiene and report food-safety concerns promptly. Cooking, open flames and high-load cooking appliances inside bedrooms are prohibited unless specifically authorised and safe.

10. Electricity, water and appliances

Switch off lights, fans, ACs and geysers when not in use. Do not alter wiring, overload sockets or use damaged equipment. Report faults, leaks and defects immediately. Usage-based charges must follow the agreed billing method.

11. Fire and emergency safety

Keep stairs, corridors, exits, extinguishers and alarms unobstructed. Learn evacuation routes and follow reasonable emergency instructions. Smoking, open flames and storage of flammable materials are prohibited in residential areas. Call emergency services when needed and inform the warden.

12. CCTV, privacy and access

CCTV may be installed at entrances and appropriate common areas with suitable notices and restricted recording access. Cameras must not be installed in bedrooms, bathrooms or changing areas. Management entry to occupied rooms should ordinarily be on notice, except emergencies or legally justified circumstances.

13. Maintenance and damage

Report maintenance requests to the warden/office. Management arranges reasonable repairs. Proven damage from misuse or negligence beyond normal wear and tear may be charged with explanation and evidence. Routine wear and tear is not chargeable.

14. Personal property

Secure cash, jewellery, devices and important documents. Report lost property immediately. Management takes reasonable security precautions; liability for loss is determined under the agreement and applicable law.

15. Health and medical emergencies

Provide an emergency contact. Disclosure of information needed for emergency assistance is voluntary. For a medical emergency, staff may contact emergency services and the nominated contact. Routine medical care remains the resident’s responsibility unless otherwise agreed.

16. Grievances and safeguarding

Raise concerns with the designated warden or management by phone, in writing or through the complaints register. Serious complaints will be escalated promptly. Retaliation against a resident raising a good-faith complaint is prohibited.

17. Notice, checkout and deposit refund

The notice period should be 30 days minimum. If the notice period is less than 15 days - 1 month Rent will be adjusted. If the notice is more than 15 days and less than 30 days, half a month Rent will be adjusted and balance amount will be refunded.

18. Changes to rules

Management may issue reasonable written operational updates, subject to applicable law and the signed agreement. Material changes to price, deposit or essential terms require appropriate agreement or notice; changes must not retrospectively remove accrued rights.
